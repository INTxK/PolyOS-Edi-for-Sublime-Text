## PolyOS Editor v3.3.4 — correct theme texture paths

- **Theme textures load again.** Sublime Text resolves `.sublime-theme`
  texture paths relative to the `Packages/` root, so a leading `Packages/`
  prefix was duplicated (`Packages/Packages/...`), every texture failed to
  decode, and Sublime painted its red/yellow "unable to decode" stripes over
  the tab, scrollbar, and overlay chrome. All image references now use the
  canonical `PolyOS-Edi-for-Sublime-Text/assets/...` form.
- The scrollbar thumb, current tab, and quick-panel textures are the intended
  dark-teal Vim-blackboard look — not corruption.

No profile or keybinding changes — just apply the upgrade.
